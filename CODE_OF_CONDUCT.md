# Code of conduct

Help make Medal Sync a respectful place to report problems, ask questions and
contribute. Assume good intent, explain technical disagreements with evidence,
and give feedback about the work. People have different experience levels and
available time.

Harassment, threats, discriminatory language, sexual content directed at
others, impersonation, spam and publishing someone else's private information
are unacceptable. Do not pressure people to share private libraries, recordings,
credentials or personal details to receive help.

This policy applies to project issues, pull requests and other project-managed
spaces. Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content, lock a conversation,
or restrict participation. They should explain moderation decisions when doing
so would not expose private information or put someone at risk.

Report harmful content using GitHub's **Report content** or **Report abuse**
controls when available; see
[GitHub's reporting guidance](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
For an immediate project moderation request, you may open an issue linking the
affected conversation without repeating abusive content or personal details.
There is no separate private project moderation mailbox; use GitHub's private
reporting controls for sensitive reports. Maintainers should respect the privacy
of everyone involved.
