Medal Sync
Source on GitHub

Sync your Medal clips between gaming PCs

Medal Sync is a free, open-source Windows companion for Medal. It keeps your clip library on your own NAS, disk or cloud drive and hands it from PC to PC. Each PC keeps its own Medal login and recording settings.

Download for Windows Read the setup guide

Windows x64. MIT license. Not made by or affiliated with Medal. The installer is not code-signed yet, so Windows may warn about an unknown publisher. Check the download and continue safely.

Where your clips live

Medal records to a fast drive backed by your local SSD, and clips upload in the background. Pick where the library is kept.

What moves between PCs

Shared

Stays on each PC

  • Your Medal login
  • Microphone and encoder settings
  • Storage and cloud sign-ins
  • The full Medal database

Switching PCs

  1. Open Medal through Medal Sync.

    This PC takes the library and mounts the clip drive.

  2. Record as usual.

    New clips upload in the background while you play.

  3. Quit Medal and wait for Ready.

    Medal Sync finishes uploads and checks every copy on your storage before it lets go.

  4. Open Medal on your other PC.

    It picks up the same library. If a handoff is interrupted, the first PC keeps the library and its clips and retries next time.

Questions

Is Medal Sync free?

Yes. Medal Sync is free and open source under the MIT license. You only need your own storage: a local disk, an SMB share or NAS, or a cloud account you already have.

How do I sync Medal clips between two PCs?

Install Medal Sync on each PC and point them at the same library on an SMB share, NAS or Google Drive. Open Medal through Medal Sync. When you quit Medal, it finishes uploads, verifies them and shows Ready; your other PC can then open Medal with the same clip library. Each PC keeps its own Medal login and recording settings.

Can I back up Medal.tv clips to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox?

Yes. Medal Sync can send verified copies of your clips to OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or any rclone destination. Google Drive can also hold the main library without a server.

Can I store Medal clips on a NAS or home server?

Yes. Medal records to a fast drive backed by a local SSD cache, and clips upload in the background to your SMB server or NAS.

Is Medal Sync made by Medal?

No. Medal Sync is an independent project and is not affiliated with Medal, Microsoft, Dropbox or Google.

Before you start

Medal Sync is experimental. Keep a separate backup of your clips: a synced library is not a backup. The setup guide walks through creating or joining a library.
