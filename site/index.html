Free · Open source · Windows

Sync your Medal.tv clips between PCs, on your own storage

Medal Sync is a free Windows companion for Medal. It records to a fast SSD cache, keeps your clip library on your own server or disk, and can back it up to OneDrive, Dropbox or Google Drive. Move between gaming PCs with one shared library, while each PC keeps its own Medal login and recording settings.

What Medal Sync does

Shared clip library across PCs

Play on your desktop today and your second PC tomorrow. One library, handed between PCs safely.

Records at SSD speed

Medal writes to a virtual clip drive backed by a local SSD cache. Clips upload in the background.

Your storage, not a quota

Keep clips on an SMB server or NAS, a local folder, or any rclone remote.

Cloud backup copies

Send verified copies to OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or several rclone destinations.

Verifies before handoff

Uploads are checked before another PC can take over. Anything uncertain stops and waits for you.

Private data stays private

Medal logins, provider credentials and full Medal databases are never copied to shared storage.

How syncing Medal between PCs works

  1. Start Medal through Medal Sync. It takes ownership of the library and mounts the clip drive.
  2. Medal records as usual. New clips upload in the background.
  3. When you quit Medal, Medal Sync finishes uploads, verifies every required copy and shows Ready.
  4. Another PC can now start Medal and pick up the same clip library.

If something is interrupted, the PC keeps ownership and its local clips so the next launch can retry.

Questions

Is Medal Sync free?

Yes. Medal Sync is free and open source under the MIT license. You only need your own storage: a local disk, an SMB share or NAS, or a cloud account you already have.

How do I sync Medal clips between two PCs?

Install Medal Sync on each PC and point them at the same library on an SMB share, NAS or Google Drive. Launch Medal through Medal Sync. When you quit Medal, it finishes uploads, verifies them and shows Ready; the other PC can then start Medal with the same clip library. Each PC keeps its own Medal login and recording settings.

Can I back up Medal.tv clips to Google Drive, OneDrive or Dropbox?

Yes. Medal Sync can send verified copies of your clips to OneDrive, Dropbox, Google Drive or any rclone destination. Google Drive can also be the main library without a server.

Can I store Medal clips on a NAS or home server?

Yes. Medal records to a fast virtual drive backed by a local SSD cache, and clips upload in the background to your SMB server or NAS.

Is Medal Sync made by Medal?

No. Medal Sync is an independent project and is not affiliated with Medal, Microsoft, Dropbox or Google.
